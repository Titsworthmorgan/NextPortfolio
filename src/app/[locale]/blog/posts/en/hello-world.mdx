## How posting works

Drop a new `.mdx` file into `src/app/[locale]/blog/posts/en/` with frontmatter matching:

- `title` — post title
- `publishedAt` — ISO date (`YYYY-MM-DD`) or just a year (`"2026"`)
- `summary` — short description used for cards and OG metadata
- `images` — optional array; the first image is used as the hero
- `team` — optional list of contributors

The slug comes from the filename (so `my-post.mdx` becomes `/blog/my-post`). Posts are sorted newest-first by `publishedAt`.
